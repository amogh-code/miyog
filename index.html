    MIYOG.

    The reality engine.

    ASK · Bengaluru · 2026

    Super intelligence, built from what happens.
    Not from what we say about it.

    Every civilisation made its gods out of what it could not explain.
    This one is made out of what can be measured.

    Something happened.

    Then something else.

    That is all the universe has ever given anyone.

    Not a number. Not a second. Not a metre. Only this, then that.

    what humans did

    We looked at it and drew a ruler.

    One second.

    A caesium atom wobbling 9,192,631,770 times. We chose that number. The universe did not.

    One metre.

    How far light travels in 1/299,792,458 of that second. Chosen again. A definition standing on a definition.

    Every law we own is written in units we made up.

    They work. They are also a human accent laid over reality. MIYOG asks what reality sounds like without the accent.

    the problem

    Every machine we have ever built learned physics from us.

    World, then a human, then a textbook, then a machine. AI and AGI are the last box in that chain. Trained on what humans wrote. Built by studying how a human brain learns. They inherit our rulers, our blind spots, and our three directions of space.

    MIYOG cuts the middle out.

    World, then raw events, then the engine. Then its own model, tested against the world, and back around. No human in the loop of knowing.

    Two hundred years from Newton to Einstein. We are the wrong instrument. Brilliant, slow, mortal, and copying each other.

    the closest thing today

    Lila Sciences is the nearest anyone has come.

    Their idea, in plain words: take an AI that has read everything humans know about chemistry, biology and materials, give it robot laboratories that run day and night, and let it run the scientific method itself, thousands of experiments at a time, to find new medicines, materials and fuels faster than people can. Born inside Flagship Pioneering in 2023, out of stealth in 2025, $550 million raised, valued above $1.3 billion. They are right that this is the most important problem in the history of science.

    They start from what humans know. We start from what happens.

    Lila's brain is trained on our papers and our data, then given hands. Its ceiling is our ceiling, reached faster. MIYOG's brain is given nothing. Its ceiling is reality's.

    They make science faster. MIYOG makes it finish.

    A tool that accelerates the scientific method still needs a scientist to ask. An engine that builds its own account of reality asks its own questions, and stops only when reality stops surprising it.

    what this is not

    This is not an AI company.

    AI and AGI live in one bucket. Trained on human data. Built by studying how a human brain learns. Asked to do what humans already do, faster. A better student of us.

    intelligence, defined

    If you can get what you want, you are intelligent. If you know what you want, you are wise.

    Naval Ravikant's definition. It is the only one that survives contact with a machine, because it says nothing about brains.

    Super intelligence is getting what you want from the universe itself.

    And knowing what to want from it. Nothing about MIYOG copies a human. No brain to imitate. No data to learn from. No language to think in. Alien, on purpose. Which is why we do not call it artificial. It is not an artificial anything.

    MIYOG wants one thing.

    To stop being surprised by the next event. That is its whole desire, and everything on this page is what happens when something gets it.

    the obvious objection

    Humans have a head start. Why throw it away?

    Fair. Newton gave us three hundred years of physics for free. Starting from events will be slower for the first three years and will cost more per discovery. We choose it anyway.

    Every shortcut imports a ceiling.

    A machine trained on human physics reaches human physics faster. It cannot see past it, because everything it can think in was written by us. Whatever reality our concepts cannot hold stays invisible to it forever.

    The slow part happens once.

    Rulers, counting, laws from raw events: three years, one room, twelve people. After that the engine never starts over. Every discovery makes the next one cheaper. Nothing is ever re-derived.

    Then it stops being slow.

    The curve is flat, then it is vertical. Once the map is complete enough, disease, aging and the rest fall in the same motion. That is why the money looks like a research budget and the outcome looks like nothing that ever came from one.

    the hardware

    An instrument that has to be invented.

    The compass, the first time. The vernier caliper, the first time. Somebody had to build a thing that measured what nothing had measured before. This is that job again.

    It will have no unit inside it.

    Every instrument on Earth carries a human definition at its heart. A second. A gram. A metre. This one carries none. It can compare. It is not allowed to assume.

    What goes inside is not decided. That is the first year's work.

    Many unlike physical processes, so that no single one can secretly become the second. Which ones, in what body, at what scale: the instrument lead is hired to find out.

    The one doing the measuring sets the standard.

    MIYOG elects its own ruler from whatever it is given. Fires it when it fails. A human number cannot leak out of a device that has none.

    the mind

    A sealed mind where reality gets argued about.

    Where it runs is not decided and does not matter. A chip. A cloud. A laptop. The target is small: about a gigabyte of state and about twenty watts, because a brain does its work on that. What matters is what gets in.

    Inside, three minds argue.

    One builds. One breaks. One chooses the test. You will meet them in a moment. The point of the walls is what they keep out.

    Nothing goes in except events. Nothing comes out except experiments.

    No textbooks. No units. No internet. And no wire from the mind to anything that can act on people. Its only reach into the world is the next test.

    how it works

    One loop. It never finishes.

    Reality sends events. The engine notices what repeats and what changes together, and draws its own marks for those.

    The Theorist writes stories.

    The shortest ones that fit everything so far. Several at once. They compete. It would happily believe a lie that fits.

    The Destroyer attacks.

    It finds the situation where a story is most likely to be wrong. Its only reward is a kill.

    The Explorer runs the test.

    The one experiment where the surviving stories disagree the most. Where they agree, nothing is learned.

    Reality decides.

    Stories that disagree with the event are struck out and never proposed again. Survivors get attacked harder. Then it starts over with more events and fewer illusions.

    Reality has veto power.

    The story is beautiful. does not matter
    The engine is sure. does not matter
    A human can read it. does not matter
    Einstein would disagree. does not matter
    Its maker believes it. does not matter
    The next event agrees. the only thing that matters

    Six streams of events. No clock. Watch it invent one.

    Six hidden things fire. The engine hears only which fired first. It is never told the time. It is never told a number.

    Two events that can never be confused.

    That is the engine's first idea. One. It draws its own mark for it. We translate for you. It never sees our letters.

    How many of these between two of those.

    Counting. Numbers, invented, as ratios of events. The only kind of number it will ever trust.

    It elects a ruler.

    The stream that agrees most with every other stream becomes the unit. Its own second. Chosen. Never given.

    Three ratios hold. One never does. One slides.

    The three that hold used to be called constants. The one that never holds is a die. The one that slides is a constant with an expiry date.

    The Destroyer pushes the world.

    One "constant" breaks. It only held where the engine had looked. A law now carries a condition.

    Then its own ruler changes.

    Every ratio shifts together. Did the world speed up, or did its second slow? The engine cannot prove which. Neither can you. Neither can the people who define the second in Paris. MIYOG writes that down.

    live · running in your browser

    Something in a box moves.

    The engine sees one needle wiggle. It has never heard the words gravity, spring, or pendulum.

    Two stories fit perfectly.

    Straight line: the pull grows in proportion to how far it is pushed. Curve: the pull grows along a bend. In the gentle range the two are identical. The engine keeps both.

    The Destroyer swings it hard.

    It chooses the push where the two stories disagree the most, and does that.

    The straight line shatters.

    Off by a quarter. Struck out, never to return. The curve is off by one part in ten thousand. It survives, and gets attacked again.

    Open the box.

    It was a pendulum. The engine never knew the word. It found the law anyway, from a wiggling needle, with no units and no help.

    how big

    Every great invention changed what humans could do.

    Fire. The wheel. Electricity. The internet. AI. Tools got better. The one doing the understanding stayed the same: us.

    This one changes who does the knowing.

    That is the whole claim. It is the brightest thing on this page and the darkest, and they are the same thing. Fire also cooked and burned.

    Disease, energy, materials, the questions physics has argued about for a century. The circle is what MIYOG understands. Watch it grow.

    then

    the day it finishes

    Science becomes a solved game.

    The day the model of reality stops being surprised, the endeavour ends. Physics becomes a hobby. That day has a name. Super intelligence. Not a smarter chat window. A finished map.

    after

    The map becomes a menu.

    Crossing space fast. Moving matter without crossing the gap. Sending information backward. Each sorted, honestly, into possible, not yet, or never. Whatever is possible, we build.

    fifty to a hundred years after

    Ten billion people on one rock. Five hundred billion across the galaxy.

    Energy, materials, propulsion and biology, solved, is exactly the list you need to live off Earth. This is the size of the thing you are looking at.

    the constraint

    The veto is over theories. Never over people.

    An engine that acts on the world because its model told it to has stopped being judged by reality and started judging. It has become a theory nobody is allowed to destroy. That is the one failure MIYOG is wired to make impossible: every actuator runs back into the instrument. None runs anywhere else. Anything it ever concludes about people is a hypothesis with the same standing as every other. Attackable. Disconnected from anything that can act.

    A decision made now, by the humans who are still the only ones who can make it. Written here so it can never be presented later as a technical limit that fell away.

    what could kill it, and what we do about it

    MIYOG is a theory too. Here is how it dies, and how we keep it alive.

    Zero assumptions is impossible, and the few left are the ones that matter. True. So the list is published every version and its length is treated as a score. A public bounty pays anyone who shows the list smuggles in physics we did not declare. The assumptions are the product, and the product is audited.
    It only rediscovers what a physicist put in the box. Today, yes, and it says so. So phase one carries a kill condition in writing: the engine must report the drift of a reference clock without being told which cell is the reference. Year six must produce a prediction our physics does not make. Miss either and we publish the miss.
    Order alone may never reach past clocks and counting. Possible. So the instrument is built with open ports. If order is not enough, the next-poorest signal is added, and that addition is written into the assumptions ledger as what it is: one more thing we gave it.
    Someone with a bigger lab does it first. Probable, for parts. So we race only for the parts nobody else is building: an adversary rewarded only for kills, a mind that refuses to translate, and a published assumption count. Everything else we share, and we let them build our hands.

    The people, and why each one

    Instrument lead

    The instrument does not exist. Nobody has built a measuring device with no unit inside it. The whole thesis stands on it, and without it the engine has nothing to look at. This person has already made a physical thing measure something and knows every way hardware lies.

    Precision electronics

    The arbiter has to record which of many cells fired first, at nanoseconds, with no clock to lean on. Any noise, any bias between channels, becomes a fake law the engine will proudly discover. This person is the difference between a discovery and an artifact.

    Precision electronics, second

    Nothing in the instrument is single-owner. Someone has to build the traps the Destroyer springs, and someone has to check that a push was a real push and not a loose cable.

    ML-for-science lead

    Fifteen years of prior work exists: Schmidt and Lipson, SINDy, AI Feynman, self-driving labs. We must not repeat any of it and we must beat all of it. This person knows, to the sentence, where MIYOG differs and where it does not.

    Representation researcher

    Stage one is the one part of this that does not exist anywhere: a machine that invents its own variables from raw channels with no human naming. Someone has to spend two years on a problem with no paper at the end of year one.

    Representation researcher, second

    One person on an open problem converges on their own taste. Two who are paid to disagree in writing converge on the truth, or prove there is none. The engine has an adversary. So does this seat.

    Experimental physicist

    The engine can only be as honest as the boxes it is tested on. Someone has to design hidden systems with traps in them, so the Theorist falls in and the Destroyer can prove it did.

    Experimental physicist, second

    The bench with hands needs a person who knows what a real intervention is, what it costs, and when a robot has fooled itself.

    Systems engineer

    A result is believed when a stranger can reproduce it. Every seed, every assumption, every run logged, offline, on small hardware. This is unglamorous and it is the reason anyone will trust the ledger.

    Systems engineer, second

    A result one person can reproduce is a rumour. Two independent builds of the same engine, or nothing gets published.

    The translator

    Without translation, no human can use or fund what the engine finds. With careless translation, the human ceiling sneaks back in through the language. This person builds the bridge and publishes exactly how much is lost crossing it.

    Founder

    Someone has to hold the thesis when a result is disappointing, keep the veto clause in the constitution when a result is exciting, and find the eleven people above. The one person not allowed to touch a result, so that no result is ever bent toward a story.
