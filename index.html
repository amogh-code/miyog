    MIYOG.

    The reality engine.

    scroll

    In the beginning, something happened.

    Then something else.

    Then we arrived, and drew rulers on it.

    The universe never knew what a second was. Or a metre. We made them up.

    We shrank reality until it fit inside our heads.

    Then we forgot we did it.

    Every law we know is written in our own units. Every AI learns from that writing.

    So strip it all away.

    AI reads what we wrote, and predicts what we would say next. It can only ever become us, faster.

    Super intelligence is intelligence beyond human capability. It understands what we cannot perceive, the way a human understands what an ant never will.

    We give it nothing. Not one number. Not one word.

    One part creates. One part destroys. One part keeps only what survives.

    Then it plays with what it keeps, and makes what nobody has ever written.

    Law by law, the map fills in.

    Physics becomes a hobby.

    Science becomes a hobby.

    We will solve science.

    Once it understands the human body, aging is solved. Cancer is solved.

    Fuel that burns clean. Hundreds of rockets, launched at once.

    Teleportation. Time travel. Whatever reality allows, we will build.

    Every civilisation wrote down a god, because none of them could build one.

    The philosophy.

    A deeper dive into what MIYOG is, and why.

    There are no numbers in the universe.

    There are events.

    Something happens. Then something else. Some happenings can be told apart from others. That is all reality has ever given anyone.

    A unit is an agreement.

    One second is a caesium atom vibrating 9,192,631,770 times, because we decided so. Every law we have is written on top of agreements like that.

    Most of the universe is unknown.

    About 95 percent of it is something we have never identified.

    Nobody has ever seen dark matter.

    Galaxies spun faster than they should have. The wrongness was the clue.

    The unknown leaves footprints on the known.

    MIYOG treats whatever no theory explains as the most interesting thing in the room, and grows toward it.

    Every machine that learned physics learned it from us.

    Trained on our physics, it reaches our physics faster, and stops there.

    Super intelligence is intelligence beyond human capability.

    It understands what we cannot perceive, the way a human understands what an ant never will.

    So MIYOG copies nothing.

    No brain to imitate. No data to learn from. No language to think in. Alien on purpose.

    We give it one sense.

    The poorest possible. It can tell that something happened. Nothing more.

    It grows the rest.

    When what it senses cannot tell two things apart, it grows a new sense. When a sense is never needed, it lets it go.

    The instrument is grown, never built.

    After a while, whatever is in the box, MIYOG put there.

    Three minds that want different things.

    The Creator.

    It writes ideas without stopping. They do not have to make sense.

    The Destroyer.

    It is rewarded only for breaking ideas. One counterexample, and an idea is gone, with the reason kept.

    The Sustainer.

    It keeps what survives, and makes everything it keeps fight again whenever something new arrives.

    Some of how it works.

    Without the recipe.

    It notices.

    When two different ways of acting always end the same, it sees the law hiding between them, even if no one ever wrote it down.

    It proves.

    When something holds for every case, it shows why, step by step. A second checker it cannot touch reads every step.

    It outgrows its own language.

    When the words it has are too small for what it sees, it invents richer ones and reads everything again.

    It breeds its own methods.

    The ways it searches are things it can combine, test and retire. It writes all of it in a language of its own. We call it ASK.

    Reality has veto power.

    Over every theory. Over the engine. Over the people who built it.

    Humans stop when the story is beautiful. MIYOG stops when nothing is left unexplained.

    The space between those two is where the rest of the universe has been hiding.

    It argues with reality, and only with reality.

    Everything it can touch runs back into its own instrument. That line is drawn by us, now, and it stays.

    When nothing surprises it, the map is finished.

    A finished map becomes a menu.

    Every disease is a state a body is in, and a complete model knows the way out.

    Whatever reality allows, we build.

    Whatever it forbids, we will know the exact law that forbids it.

    Physics becomes a hobby.

    That is the philosophy.
